The Best Salisbury Steak Meatballs with Rich Mushroom Gravy

4 servings

Ingredients

  • Ground beef – 450 g
  • Egg – 1 large
  • Breadcrumbs – 50 g
  • Onion, finely chopped – 1 small
  • Garlic, minced – 3 cloves
  • Worcestershire sauce – 1 tbsp
  • Salt – to taste
  • Black pepper – to taste
  • Olive oil – 1 tbsp
  • Butter – 1 tbsp
  • Mushrooms, sliced – 250 g
  • Beef broth – 400 ml
  • Soy sauce – 1 tsp
  • Flour – 1 1/2 tbsp
  • Fresh parsley – for garnish

Directions

  1. Mix ground beef, egg, breadcrumbs, onion, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, salt, and pepper.
  2. Form into evenly sized meatballs.
  3. Brown meatballs in olive oil, then remove.
  4. Melt butter and sauté mushrooms until golden.
  5. Stir in flour, then slowly add beef broth and soy sauce.
  6. Return meatballs and simmer 20–25 minutes until thickened and tender.
  7. Garnish with parsley and serve hot.
