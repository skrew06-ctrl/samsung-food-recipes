Italian Sausage and Tortelloni Soup

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground mild Italian sausage
  • 1 medium white onion, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 1 cup chopped carrots
  • 6 minced garlic cloves
  • 3 oz tomato paste
  • 48 to 64 oz chicken broth
  • 2 cans diced tomatoes, 14 oz each
  • 8 to 16 oz 4-cheese tortelloni
  • 3 oz fresh chopped spinach
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream, optional
  • fresh parsley, to garnish
  • 1/4 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp dried basil
  • 1/8 tsp red chili flake
  • 1 tsp hot Calabrese peppers, optional
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • salt and pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Brown sausage and drain grease.
  2. Add onion, celery, and carrots.
  3. Add garlic and a chopped red bell pepper, then mix and set aside the meat and veggies.
  4. Add tomato paste and cook it down.
  5. Add diced tomatoes and chicken broth.
  6. Add seasonings, mix, cover, and simmer.
  7. When veggies are tender, add tortelloni and cook until done.
  8. Finish with spinach and optional cream.
  9. Serve with optional sour cream, extra heat, and crusty bread.
