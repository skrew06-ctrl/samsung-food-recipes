Marinated Cheese & Salami Jar Bites Recipe

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 200 g cheese cubes: feta, cheddar, gouda, or mozzarella pearls
  • 120 g salami slices or cubes
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced or cubed
  • 6 to 8 cherry tomatoes, halved, optional
  • fresh herbs: thyme, rosemary, basil, or dill
  • 2 to 3 cloves garlic, sliced
  • Marinade: 120 ml olive oil
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp lemon juice or white vinegar
  • Marinade: 1 tsp honey, optional
  • Marinade: 1 tsp oregano
  • Marinade: 1/2 tsp chili flakes, optional
  • Marinade: salt and black pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Prepare 4 small glass jars and make sure they are clean and dry.
  2. Layer the ingredients in each jar: salami, cheese, cucumber, tomatoes if using, herbs, and sliced garlic.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the marinade ingredients.
  4. Fill each jar with marinade until the ingredients are mostly covered.
  5. Close the jars and refrigerate at least 1 to 2 hours. For best flavor, refrigerate 6 to 12 hours.
  6. Enjoy straight from the jar or pour onto a small plate.
