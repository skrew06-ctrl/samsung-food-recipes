Chili Crisp Alfredo Pasta

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 1/2 cup pasta
  • 2 to 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 tablespoon garlic chili crisp
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 1 tablespoon parsley
  • salt and pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil and garlic in a skillet.
  2. Add pasta and stir to coat.
  3. Pour in chicken broth, bring to a boil, then simmer 8–10 minutes.
  4. Add heavy cream while simmering.
  5. When most liquid has boiled away, remove from heat and mix in butter.
  6. Stir in chili crisp, Parmesan, and parsley.
  7. Season with salt and pepper and enjoy.
