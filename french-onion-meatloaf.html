French Onion Meatloaf

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground beef
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1.5 cups Gruyère cheese, shredded
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Caramelize the onions in butter over medium heat until deep golden brown, about 20 minutes. Deglaze the pan with the beef broth and simmer for 3 minutes.
  2. Mix the ground beef, breadcrumbs, egg, half of the caramelized onions, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Shape into a loaf in a baking dish and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 40 minutes.
  4. Top with the remaining caramelized onions and Gruyère cheese. Bake about 15 minutes more, until the cheese is melted and browned.
