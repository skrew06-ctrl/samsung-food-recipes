Garlic Parmesan Puffs

6 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 sheet puff pastry, thawed
  • 5 mozzarella string cheese sticks
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped parsley
  • 1 to 2 tsp truffle oil, optional
  • 1 egg, beaten, for egg wash
  • freshly grated Parmesan, for topping

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
  2. Cut the puff pastry into 24 equal squares.
  3. Slice each mozzarella stick into 5 small pieces.
  4. Wrap each piece of cheese in a square of puff pastry, pinching and rolling to form a sealed ball.
  5. Place the puff balls on a baking sheet and brush each one with egg wash.
  6. Bake 15 minutes, or until golden brown and puffed.
  7. While baking, melt butter in a ramekin in the microwave, then mix in garlic and a pinch of parsley.
  8. Toss the warm puffs in a bowl with the garlic butter, parsley, grated Parmesan, and truffle oil if using.
  9. Serve with your favorite marinara for dip.
