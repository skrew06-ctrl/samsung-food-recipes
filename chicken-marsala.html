Chicken Marsala

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 large boneless skinless chicken breasts, sliced horizontally into 4 cutlets
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped

Directions

  1. Pound the chicken to about 1/4-inch thickness. Mix flour, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Dredge the chicken and shake off the excess.
  2. Heat the olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet. Sear the chicken until golden, about 3 to 4 minutes per side. Remove and set aside.
  3. Add the remaining butter. Sauté mushrooms and shallot until browned, about 6 minutes. Add garlic for the final minute.
  4. Pour in chicken broth and balsamic vinegar, scraping up browned bits. Simmer about 5 minutes, until reduced by roughly half.
  5. Stir in heavy cream and simmer until the sauce thickens slightly.
  6. Return chicken to the pan, spoon sauce over it, and simmer about 2 minutes. Garnish with parsley.
