Beef Marinades for a Busy Week

8 servings

Ingredients

  • Philly Cheesesteak: 1.5 pounds rib eye or sirloin
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 2 tsp paprika
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 2 tsp garlic powder
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • Philly Cheesesteak: 1.5 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Philly Cheesesteak: black pepper to taste
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 2 pounds ribeye, flank, or skirt steak, cubed or in strips
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 2 tsp paprika
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 2 tsp onion powder
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 2 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: black pepper to taste
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 6 cloves garlic, minced
  • Rosemary Garlic Butter: 2 sprigs rosemary, finely chopped
  • Carne Asada: 2 pounds ribeye, flank, or skirt steak, cut into strips
  • Carne Asada: 1/3 cup olive oil
  • Carne Asada: 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • Carne Asada: 1/4 cup orange juice
  • Carne Asada: 1 tbsp onion powder
  • Carne Asada: 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • Carne Asada: 1 tbsp paprika
  • Carne Asada: 1.5 tbsp cumin
  • Carne Asada: 1.5 tsp salt, or to taste
  • Carne Asada: black pepper to taste
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 2 pounds ribeye, flank, or skirt steak, cut into strips
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 1.5 tbsp sesame oil
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 1 tbsp rice wine vinegar
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 2 tbsp gochujang
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • Korean Stir-Fry Beef: 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Beef Shawarma: 2 pounds ribeye, flank, or skirt steak, cut into strips
  • Beef Shawarma: 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Beef Shawarma: 3 tbsp white vinegar
  • Beef Shawarma: 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • Beef Shawarma: 1 tbsp paprika
  • Beef Shawarma: 2 tsp cumin
  • Beef Shawarma: 2 tsp coriander
  • Beef Shawarma: 1 tsp turmeric
  • Beef Shawarma: 1 tsp red pepper flakes or chili powder
  • Beef Shawarma: 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Beef Shawarma: 2 tsp salt
  • Beef Shawarma: black pepper to taste
  • Beef Shawarma: 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • Beef Shawarma: 1 yellow onion, sliced

Directions

  1. For Philly Cheesesteak, add provolone cheese when ready to cook so it melts into the sandwiches.
  2. For Rosemary Garlic Butter, add 5 tbsp butter cut into squares and frozen in the bag; add it to the steak when it is almost done cooking.
