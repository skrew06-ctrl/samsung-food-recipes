Better Than Olive Garden Fettuccine Alfredo Without the Stomach Ache

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 1 cup chicken stock or bone broth
  • 3/4 cup fat-free Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • cooked fettuccine
  • sliced grilled chicken breast
  • parsley for garnish

Directions

  1. Heat butter and garlic in a pan until fragrant.
  2. Add chicken stock or bone broth.
  3. Remove pan from heat and cool slightly so the yogurt does not curdle.
  4. Stir in the Greek yogurt and Parmesan until smooth and creamy.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Add the cooked pasta and coat thoroughly.
  7. Serve with grilled chicken and fresh parsley.
