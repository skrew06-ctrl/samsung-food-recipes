Rita's Italian Chicken Strips

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 3 pounds chicken cutlets
  • cherry tomatoes
  • olives
  • red onion
  • pepperoncini
  • fresh parsley
  • Italian seasoning
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • breadcrumbs
  • extra virgin olive oil
  • romaine lettuce
  • lemon salt
  • lemon juice

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees.
  2. In a bowl, combine chicken cutlets cut into strips, cherry tomatoes, olives, red onion, pepperoncini, and fresh parsley.
  3. Season the mixture with Italian seasoning, salt, black pepper, and breadcrumbs, then mix well.
  4. Spray a cooking sheet with cooking spray and add some extra virgin olive oil to the bottom.
  5. Lay the chicken mixture flat on the cooking sheet.
  6. Drizzle extra virgin olive oil on top of the chicken to ensure it gets crispy and brown.
  7. Place the cooking sheet in the preheated oven and bake until the chicken is beautifully cooked.
