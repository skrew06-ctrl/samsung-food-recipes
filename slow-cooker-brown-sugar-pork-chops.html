Slow Cooker Brown Sugar Pork Chops

4 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 pork chops, boneless or bone-in
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • salt and black pepper, optional

Directions

  1. Pat the pork chops dry. Season lightly with salt and pepper if desired.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the brown sugar and soy sauce until combined.
  3. Place the pork chops in the slow cooker. Pour the brown sugar-soy sauce mixture over the top and add small pieces of butter around the chops.
  4. Cover and cook on LOW for 4 to 6 hours or until the pork reaches a safe internal temperature of 145°F / 63°C.
  5. Spoon the sweet, savory sauce over the pork chops before serving.
