Beef & Broccoli Ramen

4 to 6 servings

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
  • 6 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce, divided
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch, divided
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped garlic, divided
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped peeled ginger, divided
  • 1 lb flank steak, thinly sliced against the grain
  • 2 (3-oz.) packages beef ramen noodles, seasoning packets reserved
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 5 tbsp neutral oil, divided
  • 1 lb broccoli, cut into 3-inch-long florets
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 3/4 cup water

Directions

  1. Whisk vinegar, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 1 tablespoon cornstarch, 1 tablespoon garlic, and 1 tablespoon ginger. Add steak and marinate 15 to 30 minutes.
  2. Boil water. Stir in one ramen seasoning packet, add both noodle packages, cook until just tender, about 2 minutes, then drain.
  3. Whisk remaining 4 tablespoons soy sauce with 1 tablespoon cornstarch. Add oyster sauce, sugar, second seasoning packet, and 3/4 cup water.
  4. Cook half the beef in 1 tablespoon oil over medium-high heat until browned, then finish briefly and remove. Repeat with another tablespoon oil and remaining beef.
  5. Heat 2 tablespoons oil in the same skillet. Add broccoli and cook 1 to 2 minutes until bright green.
  6. Push broccoli aside. Add remaining 1 tablespoon oil, 1 tablespoon garlic, and 1 tablespoon ginger. Cook 30 seconds to 1 minute, then toss with broccoli.
  7. Whisk sauce again, pour over broccoli, cover, and cook 3 to 6 minutes until just tender.
  8. Add drained ramen, beef, and juices. Toss about 30 seconds until glossy. Add a splash of water if needed. Top with sesame seeds.
