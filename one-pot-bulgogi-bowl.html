One Pot Bulgogi Bowl

2 servings

Ingredients

  • 4 oz thinly sliced ribeye raw, also labeled bulgogi meat
  • 1/3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/3 tbsp sesame oil
  • spray oil
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/3 large yellow onion
  • 1 green onion
  • 3 shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 oz rice cakes
  • 1/2 cup beef bone broth
  • 1 oz raw Korean vermicelli glass noodles, dangmyeon

Directions

  1. Thinly dice the onions, mushrooms, and green onions.
  2. In a bowl, mix the meat, honey, minced garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, and black pepper.
  3. In a large pot or Korean clay bowl over medium heat coated with spray oil, place the rice cakes, onions, green onions, mushrooms, glass noodles, bulgogi, and beef bone broth.
  4. Cover and bring to a boil.
  5. Cook until the meat, noodles, and rice cakes are fully cooked through.
